Faculty members of the College of Pharmacy, Sookmyung Women’s University, including our lab, were selected for the **Seoul ANCHOR 10 Challenge**, a nine-year research initiative supported by the Seoul Metropolitan Government. Through this collaborative project, our lab will contribute to developing innovative anti-aging and regenerative strategies aimed at extending healthy lifespan.
